## Now live

### Elimination Race

A fast, multi-round sprint that joins Battle Royale and Duels. Each round the slowest guessers get knocked out, until one player is left standing.

- **Early rounds** — shorter words, warm up and stay ahead of the pack.
- **Later rounds** — longer words, higher stakes, more players eliminated.
- **Final round** — one word, fastest correct guess takes the match.

### Play on your phone (DUELS only, for now)

Final Word now works beautifully on phones and tablets, so you can jump into a game wherever you are.

## On the horizon

### Champion's privilege

Finish a season at the **top of the leaderboard** and you'll get to pick the message winners and losers get at the end of each game. I'll review them to make sure they're not insane though. 

### A native mobile app

I'm exploring a proper downloadable app for phones and tablets, on top of the mobile web experience you have today. This one isn't set in stone and has no release date yet, but it's something we'd love to do. The main challenge is making sure the user has enough information on a smaller screen.

### Power-ups and items

I'm thinking about adding items to the game. Some of the folks who have tested the game have left some good item ideas.

## Under the hood

Not every improvement is something you can see, but you'll feel these. A few technical upgrades I'm working on:

- **Leaner live game updates** — sending less data between browser and game server.
- **Trimmed network payloads** — slimming down the data sent during a live game to just what the screen needs.
- **Behind-the-scenes monitoring** — better alerting so we catch and fix issues faster, often before you'd ever notice them.
